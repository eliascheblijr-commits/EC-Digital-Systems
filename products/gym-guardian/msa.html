Agreement overview

What the agreement covers.

This Agreement governs the structural deployment, licensing, and lifecycle maintenance of the Gym Guardian Business Management System ("System" or "Engine") by EC Digital Systems LLC ("Provider") for the Client.

Financial commitment

Subscription fees, pass-through costs, and scope.

Monthly Operational Subscription

The System is licensed under a flat-rate subscription model. The Client pays $100 USD per month for the full system capabilities as itemized in Schedule A.

Multi-Branch Expansion

Each additional branch added to the system incurs an additional $100 USD per month.

Pass-through costs

Resource scaling, storage expansion, or compute capacity beyond the standard resource cap is invoiced at cost. The subscription fee does not cover external registry renewals, TLS certificates, or infrastructure overages.

Service protocols

Legal compliance, billing, and service suspension rules.

Tax compliance

The Provider will issue an RRA-compliant EBM receipt for all setup fees and monthly subscription payments.

Client liability

The Client retains full responsibility for legal tax interpretation, submission accuracy, and official government filing.

Late payment penalty

Any balance unpaid more than seven calendar days past due incurs a fixed 5% administrative late fee.

Service suspension

If invoices remain unpaid for fourteen calendar days, non-essential application layers may be throttled and secondary services disabled.

Default & recovery

What happens if a client misses payments.

Grace period

After fourteen calendar days of non-payment, the Provider may throttle non-essential application layers.

Absolute lockout

After thirty days of unpaid balances, the system infrastructure may be placed into absolute lockdown.

Reactivation fee

Restoration requires settlement of outstanding balances, all late penalties, and a 150,000 RWF re-provisioning fee.

Intellectual property

Ownership, license scope, and data governance.

The Engine

The core application logic, database schemas, security wrappers, and indexes remain the Provider's exclusive intellectual property.

The Skin

The Client receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable revocable license to operate the user interface components.

Data sovereignty

The Client is the Data Controller and holds sole legal liability for collection, usage, and profiling of customer records.

Liability

Uptime objectives and limitations of responsibility.

Uptime target

The Provider targets a 99.5% operational availability threshold for the core Engine layer.

Upstream exclusions

The Provider is exempt from liability for outages caused by third-party cloud hosts, ISP failures, payment networks, or hardware issues.

Force majeure

Neither party is liable for failures caused by events beyond reasonable control, including civil unrest or network shutdowns.

Schedule A

Platform capabilities included in the Gym Guardian agreement.

Production UI/UX

Mobile-first interface with dynamic parallax and operational workflow design.

Omnichannel POS

Cashier terminal interface for cash and USSD-based transaction logging.

Digital Relational Ledger

Automated member ID allocation replacing legacy paper record-keeping.

Absolute Gate Tracking

Real-time physical check-in logging and member status validation.

Dual-Language Localization

Native English and Kinyarwanda support with a localization engine for additional languages.

RRA Compliance Ledger

Automated reporting estimates for VAT, CIT, revenue, and expenditure within standardized accounting outputs.

Mobile Payment Bridge

Custom MTN MoMo USSD dialer integration for secure transaction queues.

Bi Analytics

Dashboard visualizations tracking retention, traffic, and revenue across operations.

Unified Export Framework

Data export utilities for PDF, Excel, and compliance-ready reporting.

Web Hosting

Managed web hosting services with automatic backups, SSL certificates, and performance optimization.

Schedule B

Technical security and architecture summary.

Data integrity

The Engine uses HMAC-SHA256 cryptographic verification and an idempotency firewall to protect transactional consistency and prevent double-booking.

Session & authentication hardening

JWT tokens, restrictive cookie policies, session regeneration, and role-based access control secure user sessions.

Attack surface reduction

PDO prepared statements, output encoding, CSP headers, and CSRF tokens eliminate SQL injection and XSS risks.

Data lifecycle

Soft delete retention, recovery grace periods, and automated cleanup routines protect sensitive records and enforce data minimization.

Schedule C

Strategic and continuity commitments.

The Client grants EC Digital Systems LLC the right to use anonymized performance indicators in presentation assets. Private customer data remains strictly confidential, and continuity provisions protect the Client if the lead consultant is unable to maintain the system.
