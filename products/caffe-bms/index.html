Public menu

Virtual menu management

Create menus, categories, items, variants, and pricing with a modular structure that supports cafés, kiosks, and online ordering without disrupting service.

  • Public menu browsing without authentication
  • Item descriptions, variants, and real-time availability

Order and payment orchestration

Support in-person and online orders from intake through payment and fulfillment. Staff can update status in real time while management monitors throughput and payment reconciliation.

  • Queue, preparing, ready, and completed status tracking
  • Gateway-ready payment records and receipt handling

Financial ledger and reporting

Maintain a secure, append-only ledger for each transaction, expense, refund, and payment event. Export daily, weekly, and monthly insights for operations and finance review.

  • CSV/PDF reports across sales and revenue streams
  • Audit-ready financial trail and role controls
Architecture

Built for secure deployment and growth.

Frontend

Static PHP/JS interfaces for public menus, staff dashboards, and operational workflows with fast, lightweight delivery.

Backend & data layer

PHP business logic with relational storage for menu, orders, payments, and the immutable financial ledger.

Security

RBAC, HTTPS enforcement, prepared statements, rate limiting, and secure headers for safe staff and financial operations.

Containerized deployment

Docker-based deployment model with Nginx, web app, database, and optional queue workers for scaling and resilience.
